# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience or technology level.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, constructive and specific in discussions and reviews.
- Assume good intent, while taking impact seriously.
- Welcome different viewpoints and beginner questions.
- Keep issues, pull requests and chats focused on the project.
- Respect privacy and never publish credentials, private IPs, pairing tokens or other sensitive data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, threats, spam and deliberately harmful behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to **diwaskhatri@proton.me**. Please include a link or description of the relevant activity and any useful context. Reports will be reviewed and handled fairly and confidentially.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
